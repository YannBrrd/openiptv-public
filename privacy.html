OpenIPTV privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers the OpenIPTV apps for Android TV and Android phones ("the app"), published by Yann Barraud ("we"). Contact: u8813949370@gmail.com.

The short version

  • OpenIPTV is a video player. It contains no channels, films, series or playlists. You add your own provider, and only use content you are authorized to access.
  • We do not run servers that receive your data. We have no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting in the app.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device, except what the app must send to the services you choose to use, listed below.

What leaves your device, and to whom

The app only connects to the following, and only for the purpose stated.

1. Your IPTV provider (the server you entered). The app sends your provider credentials to it to sign in, download your channel list and guide, play streams and record them. The guide is also refreshed in the background, on the schedule you choose in Settings, so the app may contact your provider while it is closed. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to what they do with it. Many providers only offer unencrypted (HTTP) connections; when that is the case, your credentials travel unencrypted on the network, which is a property of the provider, not of the app.

2. The programme guide source, for M3U playlists. A playlist can name the address of its guide (XMLTV), which may be on another server than the playlist. The app downloads the guide from that address, as it would any web page: the server receives your IP address, not your credentials.

3. Trakt (trakt.tv), Android TV only, off unless you sign in. When you link a Trakt account, the app sends Trakt the titles you watch and your progress ("scrobbling"), and reads your recommendations and trending lists. You can unlink at any time in Settings. Trakt's privacy policy: https://trakt.tv/privacy

4. The Movie Database (TMDB) and Fanart.tv, Android TV only, for artwork. To show posters and backgrounds, the app looks up film and series titles or identifiers. These services receive your IP address and the titles being looked up, not your identity or provider details. TMDB: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy · Fanart.tv: https://fanart.tv/privacy-policy/

5. Google Cast (phone), only when you cast. The app gives the Chromecast or Google TV device you choose the address of the stream to play. For provider streams, that address can include your provider credentials, because providers require them to play. The cast device then connects to your provider directly. Google's privacy policy applies to the Cast service: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Another player app, only when you choose one. "Open in another player" hands the stream address to the app you pick (VLC, for example). As with Cast, that address can include your provider credentials. What that app does with it is covered by its own privacy policy.

7. Your own devices on your home network. The Android TV app runs a small local service so your phone can pair with it, send provider details and start playback ("Watch on TV"). It is only reachable on your local network, requires a pairing code, and is not exposed to the internet by the app.

Export files. When you export your favorites, resume positions and locks, the app writes a file where you choose, or sends it to your paired TV over your home network. Your providers and their passwords are never included in it.

We do not sell data, share it with advertisers, or use it to profile you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Content comes from your provider, which is responsible for its own age ratings.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because we do not receive or store your personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on request: your data is on your device and under your control. For data held by your IPTV provider, Trakt, TMDB, Fanart.tv or Google, contact them directly. You can still write to us at u8813949370@gmail.com with any question, and you have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr).

Security

Provider and Trakt credentials are encrypted at rest. Connections to Trakt, TMDB and Fanart.tv use HTTPS. Connections to your provider use whatever it supports (see above).

Changes

We will update this page when the app's behaviour changes, and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
